# The rules for living documents

The rulebook's rules for living documents, the questions file and the invariants ledger. Part of the rulebook, [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md), and frozen with it: owned by the style, changed only inside the template, never edited in a project built from it. Read before writing or changing a living document.

## Rules for living documents

- Present tense only; describe what is, never what was or how it got here.
- No dates and no changelog narration (`STATE.md` and `UPSTREAM.md` entries are the exceptions; each carries an absolute date, its last-verified stamp, and an entry older than 90 days is expired until re-verified, an in-flight `Now` entry after 30, since work that has not moved in a month is finished or stalled).
- Record intent and decisions, never inventory the tree can answer.
- A sentence in a living document is a claim, not a fact. Verify a claim before relying on it, and end every change by sweeping `STATE.md`.
- Rewrite in place; never append-and-preserve.
- `AGENTS.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`, and `docs/INVARIANTS.md` grow with the inquiry rather than against a number, saying everything as briefly as it can be said. Every other living document is bounded at 150 lines, the audit fails one that exceeds its bound, and the remedy is fission by subject. `README.md` stands outside both classes, governed by the README schema in BASELINE.md.
- The mechanical half of freshness is checked by `scripts/audit_inquiry.py`: paths named exist, a token the ignore file covers being read as prose, relative links resolve, in living documents and in the project's own records alike, no `STATE.md` or `UPSTREAM.md` entry outlives its horizon or stands unchanged in the queue for two of them, no bounded document exceeds its budget, every row of the invariants ledger has its three cells, a holder in the tree that contains its needle, and a rung from the list, every document under `docs/` is registered or is a record in a registered record folder, numbered in `decisions/`, `inherited/`, and `claims/` and dated elsewhere, every root entry has a room in the map or the baseline, a record changes only on its Status line or at a link target that comes to resolve, two claims quoting one figure at one pin agree, each manifest names exactly the current claims on its arrow and verifies only those at commits no older than their pins, a recorded claim's preserved artifacts exist, every review pass carries its shape, its boundary's completeness line, a line per stage, its prior pass, a depth word beside every key its Found section names, and a funnel line whose numbers never rise and agree with Found, and who ran each stage that ran, the last three from each rule's arrival on, every bibliography entry closes with its source's standing and the floor line, where one stands, names a legal one, names and schemas hold. Records are exempt from every rule in this section but the one on links; they describe the past, which does not rot, while a path only points at the present.

### The STATE.md schema

`STATE.md` has exactly four sections, and every entry in them is one line that ends with its last-verified date (YYYY-MM-DD).

| Section | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `Now` | Work in flight, or a condition future work must honor that no other document carries |
| `Next` | Work queued |
| `Deferred` | Work consciously postponed |
| `Blocked` | Work waiting on something external |

- An entry earns `Now` only while its work is genuinely unfinished.
- Completing work deletes its entry in the same change.
- The file is swept at both ends of every change.
- The audit caps `Now` at five entries.
- An entry in `Next`, `Deferred`, or `Blocked` whose text has stood unchanged, its date aside, for two horizons is a decision record trying to be born, so it is promoted to `Now`, written as a record and removed, or dropped; the audit reads the entry's age from history and fails it past that point, because the file's dates are last-verified stamps and cannot show it.
- Execution tracking lives here and never in a record.
- A pass over the literature queues in `Blocked` what it left to a person, one dated entry per source it could not open, search it prepared but could not run, or verification it needs, each naming what it waits on and why, a queued search naming the pass record whose Left out carries it written out, and the entry is deleted when a later pass resolves it. The horizon and the two-horizon rule above hold the queue, so a fetch nobody makes goes red rather than quiet.

In a project built from this template, `UPSTREAM.md` at the top of `docs/` is a living document of the same kind. It opens with the template and the commit the project is aligned to, and holds the entries the project has for its style under one section, `## Open`, with `Nothing open.` when there are none. Each entry is dated, kinded, pinned, and shaped as the agent guide says, is deleted when re-alignment resolves it, and expires after 90 days until re-verified. The file is free-growing, since the horizon bounds it better than a line budget would.

## Rules for the questions

`QUESTIONS.md` at the top of `docs/` opens with the aim, one paragraph naming what the inquiry is about and what would count as having answered it, and then asks the questions, one section per question headed `### N.` followed by the question in one sentence, numbered from 1 in the order they were asked. A question's section holds its decomposition, the conjectures and claims that serve it with their status words and each claim linked by number and title, and any recorded shortening of the spine, or it says `Not yet conjectured` while nothing serves it. A number is never reused. A question leaves only by being marked `Withdrawn` in its section with a link to the decision record that withdrew it, a split into successors being a withdrawal that names them, and a withdrawn question keeps its place, as a refuted conjecture keeps its record. Adding, withdrawing, or rewording a question shapes future work and gets a decision record. The audit holds the aim's presence, the numbering, and the agreement of the map with the ledger, that every claim whose status is not Superseded is linked from at least one question and that every question links a claim or says it has none, because a claim that serves no question and a question nothing serves are the two ways the map and the ledger drift apart; which question a claim serves, and whether a question is answered, stay with review. A pass record's Slice and an arrow's manifest name the question they serve as `question N`, and the audit holds that the file asks it, the pass from the rule's arrival on, since a record admits no edit. The file is free-growing, because its sections index the ledger and never restate it.

## Rules for the invariants ledger

`docs/INVARIANTS.md` lists what must stay true about the product, one row per claim, in a table whose header row is `| Claim | Held by | Rung |`. The claim is one plain sentence a reader can test. The holder is what refuses a violation, written as the tracked path in backticks, followed where the file holds more than one thing by a quoted needle the file must contain, a test's name, a contract's name, a rule's message; or the holder is the word review, when nothing decides the claim. The rung says what a green result is worth and is one of five words: impossible, where a type, an import contract, or a check over the whole tree refuses the violation before anything ships; generated cases, where a property test fails on a counterexample drawn from generated input; listed cases, where an example test fails on the cases it lists; advised, where a check names the violation on every run and a reader decides; and review, where nothing decides it. No rung claims proof, because a sampled property is not a proof and a passing test is not a theorem, and a holder of review pairs only with the rung review.

The docs audit decides the decidable half, that every row has its three cells, that every holder path is a tracked file containing its needle, that every rung is one of the five words, and that review pairs with review; it prints every review row as an advisory on every run, so a claim nothing decides cannot hide behind a green. Whether a claim is true, whether its holder holds it, and whether every claim the README and the map make has a row are review's. The template's rows are the exemplar and describe its demo. A project writes its own rows at adoption, cut from them, and the ledger is the project's own document from then on, re-adapted at re-alignment like `STATE.md` and never recopied. The shapes a claim takes recur across products, a total conserved, a value kept within bounds, an order preserved, a round trip without loss, an action refused to a role, a failure reverted whole, and the family's treasury lists them in its study of invariant binding.
