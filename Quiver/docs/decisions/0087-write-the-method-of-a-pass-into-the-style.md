# 0087. Write the method of a pass into the style

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

The host held the record of a pass to its shape, the boundary, the
strings as run, the funnel, the depth of every read, who ran each
stage, and held no technique, because naming a method was free and
mandating one was priced, and a method written for one field would
bind a child in another. So a pass was honest and not good. An agent
told to review the literature produced a pass shaped like the demo's
exemplars, single-index reads at judgment completeness, and said so;
whether it built concept blocks, piloted and fixed its strings,
tested them against seeds, logged its screening, snowballed or
checked its references depended on what it already knew. The owner
keeps a review method outside the family, written for a lab, that
runs every step a systematic review needs, and asked that a child be
able to run a review of that grade in any field.

## Options considered

- Keeping the method outside the family and handing it to the agent
  when wanted. Refused, because the law makes the demo and the guide
  what an agent cuts from, and whatever is absent from them is absent
  from a child's work when nobody says otherwise.
- Writing the method into the guide. Refused, because the guide is
  already long and the method is a document of its own kind, read
  whole before a pass and at no other time.
- Mandating the method whole. Refused, because a scoping read over
  four manuals has no string to pilot, and a rule that cannot be met
  honestly is met dishonestly; the six stages already set the form,
  the full spine as the default and every shortening written.
- Writing the field's tables into law. Refused, because the indexes,
  the noise, the exclusion reasons and the reporting checklist are a
  field's, and a clinical child or a legal one must not be held to an
  engineering lab's databases.

## Decision

A method file joins the spine beside the rulebook, `docs/METHOD.md`,
frozen and owned by the style, read whole before a pass begins. It
writes down the technique inside the six stages, existing reviews
first, the seeds, the criteria, the strings and the pilot, the owner's
licensed searches as run sheets, deduplication, screening with a
decision and a reason per record and a second look by a reader who
has not seen the first, full text with the deciding passage quoted,
snowballing, the grey strand, the checks of identifier, published
version and retraction, the completeness review, the fold with
extraction into claims and synthesis kept apart from interpretation,
the record, and the forms a pass takes as named patterns of collapse,
a falsifying pass per novelty claim among them. The paragraphs that
carry the demo's field, the indexes the inquiry can reach, the noise
its field is known for, the standard exclusion reasons and the
reporting checklist, open with the words Field binding and are a
project's own from adoption, re-adapted at re-alignment as the
tool-configuration blocks are; everything unmarked holds in every
field. The sentence that mandated no method is replaced. The method is
the spine, a form is named from the field's vocabulary as the pattern
of collapse it is, and a step skipped is skipped in writing.
The rule took the form of prose, because a technique is a sequence
of judgments no check decides, and the record lines that can be held
are held by the rules that added them.

## Consequences

An agent in a child asked for a literature review reads the method
and runs every step it can reach, hands the owner the rest written
out, and writes down what it skipped, in any field, the field-bound
paragraphs having been rewritten once at adoption. The demo owes an
exemplar pass that ran the method whole, because the demo is the
authority on dialect and today its passes are single-index reads.
