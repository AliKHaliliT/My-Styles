# 0073. Put an aim over numbered questions and bind the ledger to them

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-08

## Context

The host kept one root question at the top of the inquiry, in a living
file named for it, and the law spoke of the question in the singular
everywhere, in the spine, the pass record's Slice, and the manifest's
Serves. The demo could afford it, because a feature-sized inquiry asks
one question. A thesis does not. It states an aim and asks several
numbered questions under it, each with its own passes, arrows and
claims, and the seat claims to serve a thesis. Under the old shape a
second question had no home, nothing tied a claim to the question it
served, and the map was free to drift from the ledger. The demo's own
decomposition still linked claims 0004 and 0005, both superseded twice,
while 0008 and 0009 stood.

## Evidence

Measured on 2026-10-08. The decomposition in the question file linked
claims 0004 and 0005 as the refutation and the standing result; both
read Superseded, and the claims that stand are 0008 and 0009. No check
held the map to the ledger. The records that name the old file do so in
prose, never as a link, so no record needed the link repair the rulebook
allows. A child shaped by the family rehearsal from main, with the old
file, an inherited folder, an upstream file, a history and Windows line
endings, passed main's audit, failed this audit with one finding, the
missing living document, and passed it again after the migration below,
uncommitted and committed, with its selftest proving every rule and its
tree clean afterwards.

## Options considered

- Keeping one root question and reading a thesis's questions as lines of
  it. Refused, because a line was a phrase no rule defined, nothing
  numbered it, and a claim could not say which it served.
- Questions as records in a folder of their own. Refused for now,
  because a question is not a truth the way a claim is, its rewording
  already gets a decision record, and a fourth record kind would charge
  a feature-sized inquiry for a thesis's needs.
- A Serves line on every claim naming its question. Refused, because
  the map already binds the claim and a second copy of the binding can
  disagree with the first; the map is one source of truth.
- Exactly one question per claim. Refused, because a result reused as a
  premise elsewhere is a cross-link, and the tool decides an orphan, not
  a home.
- Nested sub-questions. Left out until a real thesis needs them.
- A status on a question. Refused, because whether a question is
  answered is review's judgment; withdrawn is the one mark no claim
  could show.

## Decision

The spine is aim, question, conjecture, evidence, claim. The file moves
to `docs/QUESTIONS.md` and opens with the aim, then asks its questions,
one section per question headed `### N.` and numbered from 1, each
holding the conjectures and claims that serve it, or `Not yet
conjectured`, or `Withdrawn` with the record that withdrew it. A number
is never reused. The audit holds the aim's presence, the numbering, that
every claim not Superseded is linked from at least one question, and
that every question links a claim or says it has none. A pass record's
Slice and an arrow's manifest name the question as `question N`, the
pass from this rule's arrival on, and the audit holds that the file asks
it. The file is free-growing. The demo asks three questions from what it
already held, the drift at ledger scale, the density of ties on real
amounts, and the filing rule's single rounding, and the family rehearsal
builds a child with the new shape.

## Consequences

A project re-aligning moves its file, writes its aim, wraps its old
question as question 1 with its decomposition beneath, links every
standing claim from a question, repairs any record link to the old path,
and names the number in its manifests; its old pass records are exempt
by arrival. The record folders are untouched. The three questions are
the exemplar a project cuts its own from.
