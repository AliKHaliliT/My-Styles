# 0100. Split the guide into documents read whole

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-16

## Context

The guide said it was read to its end before any work, and the guides
ran from six thousand six hundred to eight thousand one hundred words
across the family, three times what a reader takes in whole, because
every rule the family learned had been added to one of them as a
paragraph. A line budget of one hundred and fifty held the bounded
documents while the guide, the map, the ledger and the rulebook grew
free, and a line here is a paragraph, so the budget never measured what
a reader carries. The hard rules alone ran past two thousand words, and
the gate, the upstream report and the adoption, which a reader needs
only when delivering, reporting or adopting, sat between the rules and
the index that names every other document.

## Options considered

- Trimming the guide. Refused, because every paragraph is a rule with
  its reason, and a length rule cuts filler and never information.
- Raising the budget to what the guide had become. Refused, because a
  budget that follows the document measures nothing.
- One document for the hard rules. Refused, because the rules alone
  exceed what a reader takes in whole, and they divide cleanly into what
  the tree must satisfy and what a worker must do.

## Decision

The guide splits into documents a reader takes in whole, each a flat
file under `docs/` with its row in the index, every paragraph moved as
it stands with its links resolved from its new place and its references
to the old positions updated. `RULES.md` holds the hard rules of the
tree, `CONDUCT.md` the hard rules of conduct, `GATE.md` the delivery
gate, `ADOPTION.md` the adoption and re-alignment, and `REPORTING.md`
the upstream report; the host adds `PASSES.md` for running a review pass
and adopting an inquiry that has drafts. The guide keeps its opening,
the index, now first, and the commands, and its opening names the rules
of the tree and the rules of conduct as read whole before any work, the
rest when the work touches them. The rulebook's spine names the new
documents as living and frozen, the family audit holds the conduct,
gate, adoption and reporting documents whole and identical where the
guides shared them as blocks, and the arrow carries the package style's
five as it carries the rulebook. The split found the package and client
audits reading the whole guide for a document's name when deciding
whether it was registered, so a document the guide merely mentioned
passed as registered; registration now means a row in the index table in
every audit, and the selftests' legal plants register their files by a
row inside it. The word budget that makes this law follows in its own
record. The rule took the form of whole-file copies the family audit
holds byte-identical, because a shared document that cannot drift needs
no sentence.

## Consequences

An agent reads about four thousand words before work instead of six
thousand seven hundred, and the rest when the work needs it. A child
re-aligning recopies five documents whole and the guide's tail, and its
own guide shrinks to its opening, its index and its commands.
