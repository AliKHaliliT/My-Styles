# 0084. Record a thin literature as a finding

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

A pass that returns nine works from an index of thousands, or none,
reads to its writer as a failed search, and a writer who reads it so
widens the string, drops a criterion or searches on until something
comes back, and the record shows a corpus that was fished for rather
than measured. A review method the owner keeps outside the family
says it in one line, that a small, scattered literature is a finding
and not a failure, and asks the pass to report it as part of the
contribution.

## Options considered

- A corpus-size rule, with thresholds that switch the method when the
  count falls below them. Refused, because the thresholds are one
  field's and the host names methods without pricing them.
- Saying nothing, since the funnel already shows the count. Refused,
  because the count without the sentence is read as the writer's
  shortfall, and that reading is what moves the string.

## Decision

A pass that finds little records the thinness as its finding, in
Changed, with the funnel that measured it, and names it as a reason to
widen the next boundary rather than as a failure of the pass. The rule
stays in prose, held in review, because thinness is a judgment about a
field.

## Consequences

A corpus is what the boundary returned and the next boundary is chosen
from the record rather than from embarrassment.
