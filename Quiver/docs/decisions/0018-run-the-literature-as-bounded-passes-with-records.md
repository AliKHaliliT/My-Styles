# 0018. Run the literature as bounded passes with records

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-05

## Context

The inquiry layer had every place a literature finding lands, the
bibliography with editions named, read evidence as a claim kind, the
boundary-honesty gate item, and the question's decomposition, and no
account of how a pass over the literature is run: how the field is scouted,
how the sweep is bounded, what is checked between stages, how completeness
is reviewed against something published, how findings fold into the ledger,
and how the pass recurs. A doctorate is that recurrence, a loop between what
is known and what can be done knowing it, run dozens of times over years. An
agent asked for a literature review in this seat would land the results
correctly and improvise the process, which is the gap the adoption work had
just closed for refactors. The family's study method was the right ancestor,
built for one-shot catalogs of a whole field at large cost.

## Decision

A pass over the literature is a bounded unit that leaves one dated record in
`docs/reviews/` and moves the ledger. It serves one slice of the question,
names its boundary before it begins, the catalogs, venues, databases, terms,
and date range, and runs six stages inherited from the study method and
sized to the slice: scouting, enumeration, checks, completeness review, fold,
and resolution. Rigor is proportional in the project's existing sense. Every
stage has a line in the record, a collapsed stage says so and why in one
line, and three things never collapse: the boundary, the checks, and the
record. A pass claims its boundary exhausted only if the completeness review
ran; otherwise its findings enter the ledger as judgment and the claims
resting on them say so. The method is named from the field's vocabulary and
never mandated. The record's shape is fixed in the rulebook and held by the
audit with plants for each rule, the rulebook counts review passes as the
third kind of record, and the guide carries the stages, the collapse rule,
and the loop.

## Options considered

- A living synthesis document per slice was refused, because the ledger,
  the claims and the decomposition, already holds what the inquiry knows,
  and a second copy would rot.
- The study method's full apparatus on every pass was refused, because a
  harness that is too heavy for a narrow pass teaches the agent to
  improvise around it, and an improvised pass leaves no record at all.
- Leaving the stages unnamed was refused, because a stage that has no line
  is skipped silently, and the family's rule is that a shortening costs a
  written line.

## Consequences

The rhythm of an inquiry is legible in its records: a pass yields read
evidence and conjectures, an arrow turns a conjecture into run evidence, a
result reframes the question and opens a slice, and the next pass extends
the last from the date it stopped. A thesis chapter is written from the
ledger afterward rather than kept beside it. The demo carries one pass
record over its tie-breaking slice, judgment-grade and honest about what it
left unread, so the exemplar exists.
