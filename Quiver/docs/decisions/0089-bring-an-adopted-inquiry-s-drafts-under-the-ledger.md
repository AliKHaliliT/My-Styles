# 0089. Bring an adopted inquiry's drafts under the ledger

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

The adoption refactor classifies every tracked path, folds documents,
rewrites code into the convention and holds the gate, and it says
nothing of what becomes of a draft's assertions, references and
numbers, because the shared refactor was written for code. A research
project adopting the host arrives with exactly those, a chapter half
written, a reference list, notes with figures nobody can reproduce,
and a refactor that keeps the draft as a document leaves its claims
outside the ledger, asserted in prose and held by nothing. A review
method the owner keeps outside the family rebuilds a draft's
assurances before it reviews one, a claims ledger from its sentences,
a verified register from its reference list, a source for every
number, and flags what it cannot rebuild.

## Options considered

- Adding the step to the shared adoption section. Refused, because
  that section is one text across every style, and the other three
  have no ledger to bring a draft under.
- Leaving it to the owner's instruction at adoption. Refused, because
  the step is the same in every inquiry and a step that lives in an
  instruction is skipped when the instruction is not given.

## Decision

The host's guide gains a section of its own, before the upstream
report, for adopting an inquiry that has drafts. Each claim a draft
makes becomes a claim record, Conjecture until its evidence is quoted
with a pin or a key; each reference becomes a bibliography entry with
its standing, resolved by its identifier, and one that resolves to
nothing or to another work is held out of every claim until the owner
settles it; each number is traced to a result file an arrow
reproduces, or to a work, or becomes a conjecture with the number
among its threats; the drafts are classified in the inventory like
every path, and the adoption's record names what the ledger gained,
claim by claim. The rule stays in prose, because every step of it is
a judgment about a sentence somebody wrote.

## Consequences

A chapter written after adoption is written from records, and the
draft it replaced is a path the inventory disposed of rather than a
second source of truth.
