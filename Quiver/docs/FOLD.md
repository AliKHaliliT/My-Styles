# The Fold of a Pass

The method of a pass from its checks to its record and the forms a pass takes, the second half of [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), frozen with it: owned by the style, changed only inside the template, never edited in a project built from it. Read whole before a pass begins.

## The checks

Checks run between every stage and cost nothing. Every key resolves and every work is entered with the edition read. Every entered work's identifier, a DOI, an arXiv number, an ISBN or a stable locator for grey work, is resolved through its registry and its title, first author, year and venue compared with the entry; a mismatch holds the work out of every claim until it is fixed, because a reference that mixes a real title with the wrong authors reads as real to everyone who does not resolve it. Every preprint is searched for a published version, and the published version is cited where one exists. Every entered work is checked for retraction at its registry and its publisher's page, and the Checks line answers for it. A pass without the network cannot run these, says so in the Checks line, and names them as the owner's to run.

## The completeness review

The review is an independent look for what enumeration missed, against something published and enumerable, a review's reference list, a canonical bibliography, a numbered index, and never against a feeling. A pass claims its boundary exhausted only when the review ran and found nothing more; otherwise it claims judgment and its claims say so.

## The fold

A work a claim will rest on is read in full, whole where it is a paper and in its governing sections where it is a standard or a manual, and Found names the depth of every work; a Supported claim resting on an abstract is advised on every run until the work is read whole or the claim names the depth among its threats.

A value a claim will rest on is quoted in the claim's Evidence in full, with the key and the page, section, table or figure it came from, and a value the pass concluded rather than read is marked inferred where it stands. A classification of what the pass found is the Found table, key, depth and facets, one row per work. Nothing else is extracted, because a sheet of values beside the ledger is a synthesis that rots.

What the works say is kept apart from what the inquiry makes of it. A read-evidence claim quotes the source and names who it must convince; a conjecture interprets, opened under the question it serves; and where two works disagree, Resolution names which the inquiry follows and why. A pass that found little records the thinness as its finding, with the funnel that measured it. A completeness claim a fold makes, that no prior work does a thing, names the indexes and the window it exhausted, or is offered as judgment.

## The record

The record is the rulebook's, and its stages say who ran each step, the owner or the agent, because the steps a person judged are the ones a claim may rest on without a second look. Left out names every index not searched, every work not opened, every search written out for a person and every verification left to one, and the state file queues each as a dated entry. A cost line closes the record with what the pass spent, so the next collapse is chosen from data.

## The forms a pass takes

A form is a name from the field's vocabulary for a pattern of collapse, written in Method and shown in Stages, and no form is more than the spine with its skipped steps declared. A scoping read over works chosen by hand runs no string and fixes no seeds, and says so. A rapid pass searches two or three indexes over a recent window, with one reader and the second look on the unsure records, and declares each shortcut. A mapping pass searches for coverage over a long window, classifies every work in the Found table, reads full text only where the classification is unclear, and makes no claim about which approach works. A multivocal pass runs the grey strand beside the academic one under one scheme of facets, so research and practice can be compared. A systematic pass runs every step, three or more indexes, the snowball to exhaustion, the second look over every record, full text of every entered work and the completeness review against a published list, and claims its boundary exhausted. A tertiary pass enumerates reviews only and maps their coverage and the residual gap. A falsifying pass serves one novelty claim, is designed to find the work that would falsify it, reads each nearest neighbor in full and ends with a delta statement per neighbor, and every claim that something is new or better rests on one.
