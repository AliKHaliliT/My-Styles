# The rules for the bibliography

The rulebook's rules for the bibliography and the standing of a source. Part of the rulebook, [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md), and frozen with it: owned by the style, changed only inside the template, never edited in a project built from it. Read before entering a work.

## Rules for the bibliography

Every work the inquiry consulted is entered, because a source treated as a frame instead of an object silently vanishes from the record. A citation is self-contained: author, year, title, venue, and the edition or dated version actually used, since held knowledge preserves superseded editions without noticing. A DOI or URL may ride as a locator, but the citation must survive its death. Prose cites keys of the form `[authorYYYY]`, or, for a standard with no author's name, the standard's designation with its year suffixed, like `[ieee754-2019]`; the audit verifies every cited key resolves, and bare external links appear nowhere outside this file. Every entry closes with the standing of its source in one sentence, one of three:

- `Source: reviewed.`, a work published under the editorial control of a journal, a conference or a book publisher.
- `Source: preprint, checked YYYY-MM-DD.`, a version that has not passed review, with the day it was last checked for a published one.
- `Source: grey, first tier.`, `second tier.` or `third tier.`, literature outside those channels, tiered by how far its outlet controls it, a standard, a regulation, a specification, a manual or an organization's report in the first, a talk, a blog or a wiki in the second, a post or a thread in the third.
- `Source: retracted, noticed YYYY-MM-DD.`, a work its publisher has withdrawn, with the day the inquiry noticed. The entry stays, because a claim once rested on it and the record of that is the point, and its standing is rewritten in place, which a living file allows.

The file may declare, on one line below its opening paragraph, `Evidence floor:` followed by one standing, the lowest a Supported claim's evidence may rest on, a preprint ranking with the first grey tier and a retracted work below every floor; with no such line no floor holds, because which sources may carry a claim is the inquiry's to decide and this file's to show. The audit holds every entry to a standing and the floor line to a legal one; it advises on every run for each Supported claim whose Evidence cites a key standing below the floor, until the claim's Threats cite the key, for each Supported claim whose Evidence cites a retracted key, floor or none, until its Threats cite the key or a new claim supersedes it, and for each preprint not checked in ninety days, until its date moves or a published version replaces it.
