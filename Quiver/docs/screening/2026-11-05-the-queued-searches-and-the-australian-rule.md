own keys, the two hundred Semantic Scholar records under the index's
paper ids, and the statute under its compilation id on the register,

and the pass asked Semantic Scholar for no authors, so a dblp hit is
decided at title depth and a Semantic Scholar record's first author is
the one the DOI registry names, or unattributed where the record has no
DOI. The reason of an exclusion is a criterion's label or a standard
reason of the Boundary of 2026-10-16; a duplicate names the entry it
duplicates.
