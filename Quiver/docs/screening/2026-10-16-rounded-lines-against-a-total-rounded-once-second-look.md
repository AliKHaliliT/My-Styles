## Disagreements

1. W2763990695, Churchill 2014. The first screen said included, with
   the reason C-rule; the second look said excluded, with the reason Not
   parts-and-total; the pass settled on excluded, Wrong document type, a
   reference entry in a web encyclopedia; the rule it covers is entered
   first-hand as the regulation (the owner decided after the second
   look).

2. W649681444, Pukelsheim 2013. The first screen said included, with
   the reason C-method; the second look said excluded, with the reason
   Seat apportionment; the pass settled on included, C-method, a seed
   fixed before the strings, the standing exposition of divisor and
   quota methods as rounding rules (the owner decided after the second
   look).

3. W792139524, Giessing 2007. The first screen said included, with
   the reason C-method; the second look said unsure, with the reason
   whether it gives a parts-to-total rounding method or only a
   protection scheme needs the text; the pass settled on excluded, Not
   parts-and-total, read in full, the paper applies the entered
   controlled rounding method to protect EU aggregates from disclosure;
   the agreement of parts with totals is the method's, not the paper's
   (the owner decided after the second look).

4. W76441271, Fischetti 1998. The first screen said excluded, with
   the reason Related version; the second look said included, with the
   reason C-method; the pass settled on excluded, Related version, the
   interim report behind the entered journal article of the same authors
   and year, read in full (the owner decided after the second look).

5. W122781130, Giessing 2012. The first screen said excluded, with
   the reason Not parts-and-total; the second look said unsure, with the
   reason table-rounding method for disclosure control with no abstract;
   whether it addresses rounded cells against their totals needs the
   text; the pass settled on excluded, Not parts-and-total, the
   publisher's abstract describes consistent noise for ad hoc tables,
   not the agreement of rounded cells with their totals (the owner
   decided after the second look).

6. W7212301001, Alonso 2026. The first screen said excluded, with the
   reason Noise; the second look said included, with the reason
   C-method; the pass settled on included, C-method, the pro-rata stage
   takes the floor of each entitlement and allocates the residual,
   conserving the total; read in its section on that stage (the owner
   decided after the second look).
