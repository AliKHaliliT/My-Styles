# Running a review pass

The literature enters the inquiry the way an experiment does, through a bounded pass that leaves one record and moves the ledger. A pass serves one slice of one question, named by number in its record and small enough to hold in view, and is bounded before it begins: the catalogs, venues, databases, and search terms it will cover, and the date range it covers, so a later pass over the same slice extends it from before the date it stopped rather than starting again, overlapping it by the lag of the index it searches, because an index enters a work weeks after its date and a work that arrived after the first pass searched falls between two passes that meet exactly. The record of the pass lives in the `reviews/` folder under `docs/`, under the date it ran, the folder and its index row appearing with the first pass. What it found lives in the ledger, the bibliography, the claims, and the questions' decompositions, and nowhere else, because a separate synthesis of what the ledger already holds would rot.

A pass runs six stages, inherited from the family's study method and sized to the slice. Scouting walks the field's canonical catalogs and review papers to see where the slice's literature lives and how dense it is, and names in the Boundary the review that already covers most of the slice, with what this pass adds to it, or says that none stands near, because a pass that repeats a standing review is a pass nobody needed, and the reviews found are kept aside to seed the snowball and the completeness review. Enumeration reads within the boundary and names every work found, entering each in the bibliography with its edition. Every record screened gets one decision and one reason in the pass's screening log, a record of its own in the `screening/` folder under `docs/`, linked from the Boundary, and a second reader's decisions are a log of their own, so the funnel's contents are kept and not only its counts and a disagreement can be re-read; the raw exports the searches returned may be kept under `util_resources/searches/`, declared generated and named from the Boundary. The record's Found section names the depth each work was read at, full, abstract or secondary, so a claim quoting the work can be weighed by how much of it was read, and a pass that classified what it found writes Found as a table, key, depth and the facets it classified by, one row per work, because the classification is that pass's finding and a record is where a dated finding lives. Checks run between stages and cost nothing, since every key resolves, every work is entered, no entered work stood retracted at its publisher on the day of the pass, which the Checks line answers for, and the record has its shape by script. Completeness review is an independent look for what enumeration missed, against something published, a canonical bibliography, a review paper's reference list, a numbered index, never against a feeling. The fold turns what was read into ledger entries, read-evidence claims quoting the source in full and conjectures opened or closed in the decomposition. Resolution settles two sources that disagree by naming which the inquiry follows and why.

Rigor is proportional. Every stage has a line in the record; a stage that ran says who ran it, the owner or the agent, and what it did, and a stage that was collapsed says so and why in one line, which is the price this project already charges for shortening the spine. The stages a person judged are the ones a claim may rest on without a second look, and the rest are the person's to check before the record is relied on, which is why the word is written and not remembered. Three things never collapse: the boundary, because a pass without one is a browse; the checks, because they are free; and the record, because knowing later what was known when is the point. The boundary states the eligibility criteria before the screening runs, the seeds it fixed before any string ran and how many of them the strings found, each search string as run with the day it ran, and the funnel in one line, retrieved, screened, entered and read in full, whose last two numbers the audit holds to Found, because a claim about a search is checked against its funnel and a funnel nobody counted is a feeling. A seed is a work known relevant before the search, from the question's decomposition, an earlier pass, a review's reference list or the owner, and never from the string it tests, because a search is measured by what it was known to owe rather than by what it happened to return; an indexed seed the strings missed names the word the string lacked and the corrected string as a new run, and a pass that ran no string says it fixed none. One collapse carries a condition. A pass may claim its boundary exhausted only if the completeness review ran; otherwise its findings enter the ledger as judgment, and the claims resting on them say so. A pass that finds little records the thinness as its finding, with the funnel that measured it, because a small or scattered literature is a result about the field and a reason to widen the next boundary, never a failure of the pass to hide. The method a pass follows is the one [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) and [FOLD.md](FOLD.md) write down, the steps inside the six stages, both read whole before the pass begins, and the form a pass names from the field's own vocabulary, a scoping read, a rapid pass, a mapping pass, a systematic pass, is the pattern of collapse that form is, its skipped steps written in the stage lines; the paragraphs the method file marks as the demo's field bindings, the indexes, the noise, the standard reasons and the reporting checklist, are a project's own from adoption, and everything unmarked holds in every field.

The loop is the inquiry's rhythm. A pass yields read evidence and conjectures; an arrow turns a conjecture into run evidence; a result reframes a question, or asks a new one, and opens a slice; the next pass is bounded to that slice and to the dates since the last record over it, less the index's lag. Over years this leaves a chain of small dated records per slice rather than one review nobody can date, and a thesis chapter is written from the ledger afterward, never kept beside it as a synthesis of its own; where a chapter or a paper is kept in the tree, it cites claims by number and title and works by key, so every sentence in it traces to the record behind it. A pass may close its record with what it cost, so the next collapse is decided from data. What a pass leaves to a person, a source it could not open, a search it prepared for a database it cannot reach, a verification only full text settles, is named in its Left out and queued as a dated entry in the Blocked section of `STATE.md`, one per item, so the next pass or the person with access starts from the queue rather than from memory, and the horizon the audit keeps on that section makes a fetch nobody made go red rather than quiet. A search handed to a person is written out whole in Left out, the string in the index's own syntax, the filters, the date range and the export to take, so it is run as written and the next pass enters it as run, because a search named only as a wish is run as the person guesses it, and the guess is what the next pass inherits.

## Adopting an inquiry that has drafts

An existing research project arrives with drafts, notes, a reference list and results that no record holds, and the adoption refactor in [ADOPTION.md](ADOPTION.md) brings its tree under the law without saying what becomes of what the drafts assert. The adopting agent brings them under the ledger. Each claim a draft makes becomes a claim record, Conjecture until its evidence is quoted in full with a pin or a key, and nothing a draft asserts becomes Supported by having been written. Each reference in the draft's list becomes a bibliography entry with its standing, resolved by its identifier and compared with the entry, and a reference that resolves to nothing, or to a work other than the one the draft describes, is held out of every claim until the owner settles it. Each number the draft states is traced to a result file the tree holds, which an arrow then reproduces, or to a work, or is written as a conjecture with the number among its threats. The drafts themselves are classified in the inventory like every other path, kept where the owner decides, and the adoption's record names what the ledger gained from them, claim by claim, so the chapter written later is written from records and never from the draft it replaced.
