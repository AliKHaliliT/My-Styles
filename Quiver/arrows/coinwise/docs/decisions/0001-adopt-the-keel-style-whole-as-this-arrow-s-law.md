# 0001. Adopt the Keel style whole as this arrow's law

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

This arrow is the host's demonstration of an embedded codebase. It was begun
by transplanting Keel's exemplar bytes rather than by writing from a rule
summary, and it carries Keel's documentation spine, baseline dotfiles, docs
audit, and inert workflow, per the host's rulings on arrows. Until now Keel's
decision records sat in this folder as if they were the arrow's own, and the
arrow had no decision of its own on record, although adopting a style whole
is the first decision any project makes.

## Decision

Keel is this arrow's law, whole. Keel's decision records are carried in
`docs/inherited/`, byte-identical to Keel's at the host's pin and held there
by the family audit, and this arrow's own decisions live here from 0001,
this record being the first. Named incompleteness covers the domain trims the
README lists and never the spine.

## Consequences

An arrow extracted to stand alone carries its law and its history with it,
and a reader tells Keel's reasoning from this arrow's by the folder it sits
in.
