# Study 0004. The named vocabulary of research methodology

Date: 2026-08-27

## The question

What are the named concepts that the research disciplines use to talk about how
inquiry is conducted and how its claims are justified?

Not the findings of any field. The machinery of finding out: the designs, the
measurement apparatus, the threats and biases and fallacies, the inference
vocabulary, the synthesis methods, the reporting standards, the doctrine of
conduct, and the practices that make a result obtainable again.

The question was asked because the family intends to design a research-oriented
style, and a style is designed from evidence rather than from recollection. The
prior studies established the pattern: catalogue a field's own names, fold them
to a vocabulary, and let the disposition decide what becomes law.

## The boundary, as the owner ruled it

Eleven rulings fixed the edge before any sweeping began, and no pass was
permitted to reopen them.

In: research designs, sampling schemes, measurement and instrument concepts,
inference procedures as used, validity threats, biases, fallacies, questionable
practices, reporting standards, review and synthesis methods, reproducibility
practices, qualitative and interpretive methods with their own rigor criteria,
computational and machine-learning evaluation methodology, integrity and ethics
doctrine, and peer review as a mechanism.

Out, with the reason each was excluded. The mathematics of statistics, meaning
estimator theory, distributional results and proofs, because the operational test
is whether a researcher could be criticised for the choice and no one is
criticised for a theorem. Institutional and administrative machinery, meaning
grants, tenure, governance, ethics-board paperwork and procurement, because the
doctrine those bodies apply is portable and cited in method sections while their
paperwork governs an organisation and rots with each institution's forms.
Products, tools and software, because the test that works is whether a thing is a
specification anyone can apply or an artefact someone sells. Bibliometric league
tables and ranking systems, while the indicator families stay in as objects of
the critique that studies their distorting effect. Substantive domain content,
including named datasets and benchmarks. Research training and the pedagogy of
methods, except where a named framework functions as a conduct standard.

Discipline-specific designs are IN, distributed by function rather than gathered
into a discipline, because a named design is canonical vocabulary whichever field
coined it. Qualitative and interpretive methodology is IN at full parity with
quantitative methodology, including its own standards of rigor, because
excluding it would describe half the field while claiming the whole.

Where two live naming cultures name one phenomenon differently, BOTH names were
entered without early merge, and the fold united them into single entries
carrying both names. Forcing that merge at the sweep would have lost the
difference the two literatures actually maintain.

## The method, and where it departed from the written one

The study followed the treasury's six stages: scouting, enumeration, mechanical
checks between every stage, completeness review, the fold, and resolution.

The deliberate departure was the enumeration shape. The method had been rewritten
before this study so that a sweep emits NAMES rather than finished entries, with
the gloss written once at the fold, because a gloss written at the sweep is paid
for twice. This study is the first test of that change and it held. The sweep
wrote four-field name lines carrying a name, its alternate names, a flag for
whether the thing is applied or definitional, and a short disambiguator only
where a name is obscure or double-sensed.

The applied-or-definitional flag recorded at the sweep survived four stages and
still shapes the deliverable, which is the strongest evidence that naming first
and glossing last loses nothing.

## The funnel

| Stage | Count |

| Scout's density estimate, summed midpoint | 5,055 |
| Enumeration, 25 territories | 18,804 |
| Completeness review, 25 reviewers | +4,120 |
| Catalogued | 22,924 |
| The fold, 13 families at an eight-to-one budget | 2,908 |

The scout underestimated the subject by a factor of 3.7 and not one of the
twenty-five enumeration passes came in under its estimate. The reason is
structural rather than careless: a density estimate is produced by the same
knowledge that would do the sweeping, so it is biased downward everywhere and
biased downward hardest exactly where the estimator knows least. Three
territories were flagged before the sweep as likely to be UNDER-swept because
their canon sits outside the shelves general methods writing draws on, and all
three came in among the densest in the study once their passes were told to walk
the standards documents definition by definition.

The catalogued figure counts names. The reachability figure counts names and
their alternates against a corpus of 40,661, because an entry carries many names.

## How verification was done, and its limits

Checkers were written before the work they judged and run outside the pass that
produced it, because a pass grading itself reports what it intended. Each was
proven against planted defects, since a check that never fires and a check that
cannot fire look identical.

Every reviewer held the COMPLETE name index of its own territory. Measured
restatement across twenty-five reviewers was 22 names of more than 5,000
proposed, under half a percent, and seven of those twenty-two were one reviewer's
deliberate renamings which carry the old name as an alternate by design. Every
single reviewer overestimated its own restatement rate. The series' earlier
lesson was that self-reported error runs lower than measured error; given the
complete index it runs consistently higher, so that lesson was about reviewers
working blind rather than about self-report.

Three limits are stated rather than buried.

Every enumeration pass worked from held knowledge and could not fetch a source.
The honest claim the sweep supports is that its names are canonical, not that any
source is exhaustively covered. The review stage is where that changed, because
reviewers could fetch. One walked a seventy-two-entry online bias catalogue and
found sixty-seven of seventy-two present. One downloaded a ninety-page standards
document and found roughly a hundred and ten enumerated items absent. One walked
a live registry of 702 records and found 205 named instruments missing. One
pulled a book's full text and worked its index of 112 fallacies to exhaustion.

One group of twenty-one entries rests on UNVERIFIED RECALL. Three passes chased
its source, a table in a paywalled chapter with no open reproduction anywhere
reachable. Its grouping is unconfirmed and one of its entries is a suspected
non-member. Those names are canonical and their arrangement is not verified, and
a study whose worth is accurate evidence cannot present recall as transcription.

Some enumerations cannot survive a forty-word gloss and do not claim to. One
family folded roughly 110 enumerated survey disposition codes into six entries
plus their governing apparatus. The codes are all reachable as alternate names,
and the prose carries the category structure and the decision-bearing members.
The full enumeration lives in its source.

## What the study found

**The applied gradient is the central finding.** Sixty-eight percent of
catalogued names are things a person applies and pays for, and the rest end at
definition. The split is not uniform, it is a gradient, and it runs from 7
percent applied in the bias catalogue to 94 percent in reporting guidelines, with
philosophy of inquiry at 22 and trial architecture at 85. The closer a territory
sits to DOING, the more of it is priced; the closer to JUSTIFYING, the more ends
at definition.

Resolution sharpened this. The price is real for everything chosen, and the
CURRENCY differs: a reporting guideline is priced in effort, and a code of
conduct is priced in what it forbids and whom it fails to bind. The finding is
not that some things are free and some cost, but that everything chosen costs
something, denominated in whatever that kind of thing actually takes.

**An exhaustion claim is worth only what its boundary is worth.** Sixty-seven
claims that a region had been swept exhaustively were tested and thirty-four were
false, and they sort perfectly by one criterion. A pass can be trusted when it
claims to have finished a list SOMEONE ELSE published and numbered, and such
claims verified true again and again, down to obscure tails: twelve terms of one
metrology chapter, eighteen of another, nine attributes of one set, thirty-five
biases by stage, four validity threat tables row by row, thirteen of fourteen
enumerated criteria sets in one family. A pass cannot be trusted about a region
it DELIMITED ITSELF, and every such claim failed, sometimes by twenty names,
because no external boundary exists that it could have reached and the claim
reports only that the pass stopped finding names.

The rule was then used prospectively rather than retrospectively. Reviewers were
told in advance which of their own claims named an external list and should
verify honest, and which named a self-drawn region and should come up short.
Every prediction held.

**But verifying claims measures fidelity, not coverage.** One family verified
thirteen of fourteen claimed lists true while eight equally closed, published,
numbered lists it had never opened were wholly absent. A review that only audits
claims returns a reassuring result and leaves the real gaps untouched. The losses
sit in the lists nobody opened, and a reviewer must be sent to find those rather
than to check the ones already named.

**A fold budget limits ideas, not names.** Survival ranged from 38 to 95 percent
across families folding at the same eight-to-one budget, and one variable
explained it at a correlation of 0.95: alternate names carried per entry. The
budget caps ENTRIES, and an entry carries unlimited alternates at no cost, while
gloss prose is bound by the word cap. Five families reasoned this out and kept
twice as much as five that did not. Repairing the five raised overall
reachability from 60 to 81 percent while adding no entries and changing no
headword and no gloss.

The corollary is that naming a member is not enough; it must be named in the form
a reader will search. A gloss reading "the canons of agreement, difference,
residues and concomitant variation" holds all four canons and matches none of
their real names. Alternate names are stored in lookup form by construction,
which is why they are the right home for a merged entry's members.

That technique has two boundaries, both found by the passes using it. A neighbour
is not an alternate name, and an opposite certainly is not, because an entry
reading "ex ante evaluation, also ex post evaluation" asserts that a thing is
another name for its own opposite. Members and synonyms belong in the alternate
list, and neighbours and opposites belong in the gloss.

## Failure modes this study found, none of them visible to any check

Each was found by a person reading, and each is recorded because the next study
will meet it.

**The enumerated interior.** A pass enters a source's headline vocabulary and
stops at the numbered list one level down. It then feels it covered the source,
because it entered the names the source is famous for. The worst instance took
seven category names from a standards document and missed roughly a hundred and
ten enumerated items beneath them, while taking a different interior of the same
document down to its leaves.

**The invisible source.** A pass working from a document treats it as the frame it
looks through rather than an object it can name, so the document's own name never
becomes an entry even where the entry rules plainly admit it. Eleven central
documents were absent this way. Part of the cause is a name clash, since four
works share a name with the concept they introduced, so the pass entered the
concept, the slot looked filled, and the book was never entered.

**Non-members and misattribution.** A pass reconstructing a known numbered list
from memory does not only drop members, it can ADD one, so an exhaustion claim
can be wrong in both directions at once. Misattribution is the same failure with
better camouflage, where a real item is filed under the wrong author's framework
and every individual word is true.

**Conflation, which the fold creates.** Merging is what a fold does, so it is the
stage most likely to join two things that differ. Twenty-six were found and
repaired. The name exists, the concept exists, the entry is well formed, and only
the parent is wrong.

**Claim migration.** A drop applied by deletion alone is not enough, because the
dropped item's claim can move rather than die. One struck entry's misattribution
survived inside the gloss of the thing it misdescribed, where it read as that
entry's own content.

**Stale editions.** A catalogue built from held knowledge silently preserves
superseded editions. Two entries carried a code's retired wording, one set of
grounds sat one revision behind, and one badge vocabulary's annotations described
a convention its standard had reversed six years earlier. One genuinely missing
term was absent precisely because the study held an old edition of a document it
already had.

## What the instruments cost, which is the hardest lesson

Ten defects were found in this study's own checkers and contracts, every one of
them the same shape: a pattern that assumed something the real data violates.
Eight produced false alarms or noise. Two damaged the deliverable.

A first-person rule matched a standalone Roman numeral, so it failed Phase I,
Type I error and I-optimality as machinery leakage. Rather than fail, one pass
renamed those to Arabic and dropped one name outright, then reported exactly what
it had cost. A gloss cap deleted a real test name from a gloss to reach forty
words. A repair that removed an alternate name left the surviving alternates
without their connector, and the entry shape then swallowed them into the
headword, so four entries silently stopped being indexable under their own names
while the checker reported clean.

**A check that makes a worker damage the deliverable is worse than no check.**
That is the central lesson about instruments, and it is the family's own rule
proving itself, that a check may never imply more than it decides. The
corollaries are that a rule fighting real work must be changed in the open rather
than routed around, that a formatting cap must cut filler and never a name, and
that a repair must be verified for its side effects rather than for its intent.

The advisory tier earned its place by measurement. Five pairs of names in one
family differ only in capitalisation and all five are two real things, including
the central confusion between two measurement cultures. The first version of the
duplicate check folded case and would have failed all five, forcing a pass to
normalise or delete one of each pair. An advisory also surfaced two genuine
conflations that no gate could have decided.

One further finding bears on how such a tier is briefed. An advisory decides
nothing and gates nothing, and passes silenced them anyway, one editing an entry
purely to stop a note firing. **An advisory that a worker avoids is functionally
a gate.** The rule was right and the briefing was missing: whoever meets a
warning has to be told that warnings are answered rather than silenced.

## What resolution settled

Seven hundred and seventy-one names were claimed by more than one family. The
first 555 were ruled by two independent passes reading every pair of entries in
full: 448 ONE HOME, 96 TWO SENSES, 11 PARENT AND CHILD, and none left undecided.
The remainder arrived with the alternate-name recovery and are recorded for a
later pass.

The distribution was the opposite of what was predicted, and the explanation is a
verdict on this study's own family design rather than on its fold. The great
majority of matches are one thing entered twice by two families handed
overlapping headings, with dozens of near-verbatim glosses. Eleven family pairs
were given near-identical headings, and settling those eleven would resolve more
of the list than any name-by-name ruling.

So the count of cross-family duplicates measures how well the FAMILIES were
drawn. A resolution stage ruling mostly ONE HOME is reporting that its families
overlap. The rulings are recorded with each name's canonical home rather than
mechanically applied, because a duplicate costs a reader a second look while the
root cause sits in the family boundaries, and deduplicating would be cosmetic.

## The boundary that was excluded, in one line

Everything the eleven rulings above put outside, and in particular the mathematics
of statistics, the institutional machinery of research, named products and
datasets, and journal ranking systems, was excluded by decision rather than by
oversight.
