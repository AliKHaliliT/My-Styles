# Shared names

Living. A name that two or more studies carry in bold is listed here with the
studies that hold it and one word for what the sharing means. The word `same`
says the studies name one thing and price it separately, so a reader weighing
that thing reads every holder's entry before deciding. The word `different`
says one word names different things in different studies, so a reader picks
the sense before reading further. The rows are held by the treasury audit at
the repository root, which computes the shared names from the findings files
and fails when a row is missing, stale, misplaced or wrong about its holders.
The word in the third cell is review's, chosen by reading every holder's
entry. The README's ledger links every study.

A study's findings are its numbered files after the research record. A bold
name is compared lowercased, with its whitespace collapsed and trailing
punctuation dropped, and a name of one or two characters is not counted, so a
two-letter acronym never makes a row. Overlap with study 0001 is expected,
since the vocabularies name locally what the primitives name in general, and
its rows stand here so the reader finds the primitive's price beside the
field's. The table grows with every study and has no line budget, because an
index that drops rows to fit stops being an index.
