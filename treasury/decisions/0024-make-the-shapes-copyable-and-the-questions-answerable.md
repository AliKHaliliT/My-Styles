# 0024. Make the shapes copyable and the questions answerable

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-10

## Context

The owner asked whether anything should be done about intention being
misread, in the law and in the turn-by-turn exchange with an agent, given that
weaker models misread more. The week's misreadings all shared one shape, a
real ambiguity resolved literally, and each fix had added a decidable form.
Three places still asked for interpretation: the upstream entry's shape lived
in prose alone, a question to the owner had no form, and record filenames had
grown to ninety characters against a rulebook that said short, which a deep
Windows clone refused.

## Decision

The entry's shape is carried as a fenced block in every guide. A question to
the owner is numbered options with the recommendation marked and the action
each implies, and any other reply is restated in one sentence before it is
acted on. Record filenames stay within seventy-two characters, held by the
audits for records added after the rule arrived. Nothing lowers the register
of the law, and the read-back of a new rule past a weaker model before it
lands remains the maintainer's drafting practice rather than a gate. Each
style carries its own record.

## Consequences

Fewer sentences to interpret and more bytes to copy, on both sides of the
conversation.
