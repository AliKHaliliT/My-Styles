# 0032. Dispose of the upstream entry of 2026-09-17 on record links

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-18

## Context

One project sent the same defect entry from three of its seats, each
aligned at `ec94df4405f3`, under the styles for a package, a server, and a
client. Its records cite the records of sibling seats vendored beside it,
thirty-two links across twenty-seven records were written one directory
level short, and the docs audit refused the repair as an edit beyond the
Status line, which is the rule working as written. The entry asked for a
remedy short of superseding a correct decision, and for a link check over
records that the repair clause would make fair.

## Decision

Kept, as one law with two clauses in every seat. A record may be edited at
a link target alone, and the docs audit decides it, holding that the
changed lines differ inside link targets only and that every new target
resolves. Every docs audit holds every relative link in a record of the
project's own to resolve, the inherited folder excepted, since those are
checked where they were written. The records are Keel 0070, ArchetypeCore
0067, Helm 0071, and Quiver 0047, the arrow inheriting Keel's.

The entry's claim was tested against the family's own trees before it was
kept. The link checks read the living documents alone in all four audits,
the immutability check judged every changed line alike, and no record in
the family carried a dead link, so the rule reddens nothing here and would
have caught the project's defect on the day it was written.

## Consequences

The project repairs its links under the clause at its next re-alignment
and deletes the entry from all three upstream files. The treasury's
erratum of 2026-08-24, granted by the owner's word, is the last repair of
its kind that needed one.
