# 0018. Dispose of the upstream report of 2026-09-08 on the claim ledger

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

An inquiry project aligned at `83676033f377` sent two entries about the
Quiver claim ledger, nameless, dated, one letter for the inquiry layer with
its arrows reporting separately, and closing with nothing held locally, which
is the report shape ruled on the day before working on its first day. Both
entries were tested on the family's tree. The first, that the law forces a
full supersession on every movement of an arrow even when no figure could
have changed, is visible in the demo itself, where seven of nine claims are
superseded and the current one says its claim is unchanged. The second, that
evidence observed once at a price has no place the audit can read, is a gap
records 0007 and 0008 had walked up to and left in prose.

## Decision

- Adapted: the supersession churn. Supersession is reserved for a change of
  figures, evidence, or belief, and a movement whose figures reproduce is
  answered by a verification line in the arrow's living manifest, which the
  audit reads as the point movement is measured from. The report's
  in-record verification line was refused because the Status line is a
  record's only legal edit, and its audit-runs-the-experiment option because
  an audit never runs experiments. Quiver record 0024.
- Adapted: recorded evidence. A `Recorded:` line in the Evidence section
  declares the observed-once kind and names what was preserved; the audit
  never advises such a claim stale and holds that the artefacts exist. The
  report's declared kind on every claim was narrowed to the new kind alone,
  because the existing claims are immutable. Quiver record 0025.

## Consequences

The reporting project reverts nothing, stops re-pinning, writes verification
lines from its next movement on, and may supersede its paid claim once to
give it the Recorded line, or wait for the next advisory. The demo arrow's
manifest carries the first verification so the exemplar exists.
