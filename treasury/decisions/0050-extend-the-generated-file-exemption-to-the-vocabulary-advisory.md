# 0050. Extend the generated-file exemption to the vocabulary advisory

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-17

## Context

Ruling 0044 placed a file a machine wrote and the tree keeps as written
outside the em dash count and the spelling advisory, the two checks that
had met such a file. The host's first search pass then kept the exports
its searches returned, declared generated as the ruling requires, and
every seat's third check of the prose law, the vocabulary advisory,
named the index's abstracts on every run, thirty-three lines of words a
person never wrote for the project.

## Decision

Ruling 0044 is widened. A file declared `linguist-generated` in
`.gitattributes` stands outside the vocabulary advisory as it stands
outside the count and the spelling advisory, in every seat's audit and
in the root workflow's prose step, and the shared prose law and the
rules of conduct say so. Stripping the abstracts from the exports and
dismissing the hits landing by landing were refused. The package seat's
0113, the server seat's 0115, the client seat's 0109 and the host seat's
0101.

## Consequences

No check of the prose law reads a declared file, and the next pass that
keeps its exports lands without a dismissal for them.
