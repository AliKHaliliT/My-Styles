# 0011. Dispose of the doctoral repository's upstream report

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-03

## Context

A doctoral research repository was refactored against the family by an
agent on a smaller model with a medium reasoning budget, and it returned
the family's first real upstream report, six entries in one folder, each
written as a lead with the decision records checked first. The owner also
saw what the report did not say: the docs zone came back with the spine
beside foreign folders, the code kept its inherited docstrings, and the
agent could not say when alignment was complete. The family's rule is that
a lead is verified before it is adopted, so each entry was tested against
the family's own trees before anything moved.

## Decision

Kept and built into the styles: the layout audit degrading silently (true
in our own code, now it reports what it held and fails on a shape it
cannot hold); record immutability unchecked (true, now checked from the
commit that introduced the check forward); dated briefings falling between
species (true, any dated document under docs/ is now a record for
immutability); figures never held against each other (true, now
addressable and held consistent per pin, with a reproduce command per
arrow).

Adapted, the remedy kept and the diagnosis corrected: the Dependency Rule
blind spot. Probing grimp 3.15 against Keel's tree showed nested bare
directories fully traversed under the configured portion roots, so the
reported hole does not exist here and was most likely a new top-level
portion or a different tool version; the suggested guard, that the graph's
module count equals the disk's, was adopted regardless, because it catches
that case and any future one. The prose law's missing adoption clause was
adopted with the opposite default: the corpus comes under the law at
adoption and converges as tracked debt, not when someone next edits it,
with the hashed-data exclusion kept by name.

Refused: the seam rule's proposed exception. An invariant with no
observable output is observed through a counting fake at the seam it
crosses, and where no seam exists the invariant is asking for one; the
rule's text now says so, and no exemption is added.

Beyond the six, the review named what let the refactor stay half done:
the audit never looked inside docs/ subdirectories, nothing verified that
every existing directory was drawn, docstring content was review-held past
presence, and no adoption procedure or definition of done existed. All
four are closed in the styles' records of this date.

## Consequences

The reporting repository reverts its local immutability check and its
scoped prose gate in favor of the template's versions, adopts the module
count guard, drops the seam exemption, keeps its hashed-data exclusion by
name, and re-aligns to the template commit the maintainer's reply names.
The family gains its first tested account of adoption by a weaker agent,
and the lesson it recorded is that a rule with no checker behind it is the
first thing such an agent leaves alone.
