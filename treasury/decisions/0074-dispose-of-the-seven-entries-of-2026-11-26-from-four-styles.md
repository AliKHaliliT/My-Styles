## Options considered

- Landing the changes to every seat one seat at a time. Refused, because
  the spelling advisory, the queue-age rule and the reading of an
  ignored path are shared law, and a law lands in every seat it binds at
  once.
- Leaving the client's count of days as it stood, since no entry named
  it. Refused, because the proof the fourth entry asks for plants an age
  at its edge, where that count is a day off for an hour a day on a
  machine that keeps daylight saving.

## Consequences

project whose bibliography or living documents hold keys outside the
form renames them then, one that imports a slice's own files by the
alias from inside the slice moves to relative paths, and one that keeps
storage behind a link ignored by a directory pattern names the link
itself.
