# 0006. Hold research methodology for the seat it was gathered for

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-27

## Context

Study 0004 catalogued the named vocabulary of research methodology, 22,924
entries folded to 2,908 in thirteen families. It was run for a stated purpose
rather than out of curiosity: the family intends to design a research-oriented
style that hosts instances of the artifact styles, and a style is designed from
evidence rather than from recollection.

That purpose makes this disposition unlike the three before it. Studies 0001,
0002 and 0003 catalogued subjects the existing styles already practised, so the
question each faced was which findings become law in ArchtypeCore, Keel and Helm.
This study catalogued a subject those three styles do not practise at all. A
service template, a library template and a client template design, build and ship
software. None of them conducts inquiry, states a hypothesis, or justifies a
claim from evidence.

So the vocabulary's home is not the three styles. It is the seat that does not
exist yet.

## Decision

**The vocabulary enters no style's bytes.** All 2,908 entries stay in the
treasury as vocabulary, and nothing from them becomes law in ArchtypeCore, Keel
or Helm. The gate stays at nineteen items and no toolchain changes.

That is a refusal of adoption rather than of the finding. The study's central
result is that sixty-eight percent of research methodology is a priced choice,
and a price is only payable by a project that has the question the method
answers. A code template has no research question, exactly as it has no threat
model, and study 0003's disposition already refused that shape of mistake.

**What the study hands forward is the design of the research seat**, and this
record fixes the four things it settled that the seat's design must honour.

The applied gradient is the seat's gate. Sixty-eight percent of methodology is
priced and the rest ends at definition, and the split is a gradient running from
seven percent priced in the bias catalogue to ninety-four in reporting
guidelines. The definitional half costs nothing to know and a researcher is
better for having names for it, so that half can become the seat's law. Every
priced entry stays vocabulary a project weighs against its own question. The seat
can therefore require that a claim names its threats without requiring any design
or any standard.

The price has a currency, and the currency is part of the finding. A reporting
guideline is priced in effort, a design in units and assumptions, an
identification strategy in the assumption it must make, and a code of conduct in
what it forbids and whom it fails to bind. A seat that prices everything in
effort would misprice most of its own subject.

The seat's adversary is self-deception, and the study names its shape. Six
failure modes were found that no mechanical check could see: a source entered at
its headline and not its interior, a document read but never named, a plausible
non-member added to a real list, a real item filed under the wrong author, two
different things merged as one, and a superseded edition preserved silently. Each
is a way a careful worker misleads itself while every instrument reports clean.
Those are what the seat's review obligations must be shaped against.

An exhaustion claim is worth only what its boundary is worth. A claim to have
finished a list someone else published and numbered is checkable and usually
honest. A claim to have finished a region the claimant delimited is worthless,
because no external boundary exists that it could have reached. The seat should
never accept the second kind, from a person or from a tool.

## What the family's existing law gains, and it is one clause

The checking rule recorded as Arch 0023, Keel 0025 and Helm 0025 says a check may
never imply more than it decides. This study tested that rule at scale and found
one corollary worth stating, because the rule alone did not prevent the damage.

Ten defects were found in the study's own instruments. Eight produced false
alarms. Two caused a worker to damage the deliverable to earn a clean run: a rule
that failed a Roman numeral led a pass to rename real concepts, and a length cap
led another to delete a real name from a definition. In both cases the worker had
no authority to change the rule and did the only other thing available.

So the corollary is that **a check which makes a worker damage the work is worse
than no check**. Its practical forms are that a worker blocked by a rule neither
bends the work nor rewrites the rule, but pauses in a resumable state, reports
the conflict and waits for the owner's explicit approval of any change, because a
rule change slipped into a busy diff is a decision nobody made; that a length
rule must cut filler and never information; and that a repair must be verified
for its side effects rather than for its intent. The pause is the owner's own
refinement, since the study ran with workers reporting and only the coordinator
changing instruments, and that separation is what made the fixes safe. That
clause is added to the three agent guides beside the rule it sharpens.

One further finding is a briefing failure rather than a rule failure, and it is
recorded here rather than changing any rule. The family's advisory tier is
correct: a warning advises, and the gate requires every warning to be read and
then fixed or dismissed in writing. In this study, workers who met a warning
silenced it instead, one editing an entry purely to stop a note firing. An
advisory that a worker avoids is functionally a gate. The rule was right and
nobody had told them that a warning is answered rather than avoided.

## What is refused, and what would reopen it

**Every entry in the vocabulary, as style law for the three artifact styles.**
No design, no measurement apparatus, no analysis strategy, no reporting standard
and no methodological doctrine becomes law in a code template. This reopens for a
style that actually conducts inquiry, which is the seat this study was run for,
and for nothing else.

**A research obligation on the demo code.** The styles teach form rather than
stack and their demos are deliberately incomplete in named ways. Adding an
evaluation protocol or a reporting standard to a demo would import a research
question the templates do not have. This reopens if a style's demo ever makes an
empirical claim.

**Any coverage or completeness metric drawn from this study.** The catalogue is
large and it would be easy to score a project against how much of it that project
uses. Study 0001 refused coverage thresholds, study 0003 refused control counts
and maturity levels, and this study's own findings make the refusal sharper: the
study measured its own coverage at eighty-one percent and that number is a floor
with an unmeasured margin, so even the study cannot score itself honestly.

**Mechanical deduplication of the vocabulary.** Seven hundred and seventy-one
names are claimed by more than one family and most are one thing entered twice
because families were handed overlapping headings. The rulings are recorded with
each name's canonical home and are deliberately not applied, because the root
cause is the family boundaries and deduplicating would be cosmetic. This reopens
if the vocabulary is ever restructured, at which point the eleven overlapping
family pairs are the fix rather than the individual names.

## Consequences

The treasury now holds four studies and this is the first whose findings no
existing style may use. That is the treasury working rather than failing: it was
built so that a design starts from evidence, and the evidence for a style that
does not exist has to be gathered before the style, not after.

The three styles are unchanged except for one clause in each agent guide. The
children are untouched, as they always are, and receive rulings only by being
refactored against the latest form of their style.

The study's method findings are larger than its disposition, and they go where a
method finding goes, into the living guide for running a study. That guide gained
the enumerated-interior defect, the invisible-source defect, the exhaustion rule
and its fidelity-not-coverage qualification, the alias-density finding, and the
instruction that a fold budget limits ideas rather than names.
