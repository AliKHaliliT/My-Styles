# 0020. Keep upstream entries in one living document until they resolve

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

Rulings 0015, 0016, and 0019 built the upstream report in three increments
over three days: a folder of immutable letters, then replies and resolution
records beside them, then names and a duty to announce them. The owner could
no longer tell from a project's tree whether anything had gone upstream or
what had become of it, and said so. The design had treated pending work as
history.

## Decision

The three rulings are superseded. A project built from a template carries
one living document, `UPSTREAM.md` at the top of `docs/`, of the same kind
as STATE: one Open section holding entries or the words Nothing open, each
entry dated, kinded, pinned, and shaped by four labeled parts, nameless as to
the project, written when the work that produced it closes and named in the
closing note, and resolved at re-alignment against the new pin, the
template's records, and this treasury's dispositions, by deletion, with kept
divergences becoming the project's own decision records and entries older
than ninety days re-verified or made the project's own. No reply is owed and
none is filed. The audits hold the schema and the horizon, the demo arrow
carries the exemplar, and in the host seat each arrow carries its own file.
The improvement and defect kinds, the nameless rule, and the treasury's
dispositions by pin and date are kept from the superseded rulings. Each style
carries its own record of the rule.

## Consequences

One file per project, read like STATE. The record folders hold records
again, and the owner learns what went upstream from the closing note and the
file, never from a folder.
