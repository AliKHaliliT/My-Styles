# 0023. Dispose of the upstream entry of 2026-09-09 on the state queue

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-10

## Context

An inquiry project aligned at `e069bab89f81` sent one entry in its upstream
file, the first to arrive in the living-file form: its Next section had
reached eleven entries while Now held one, every entry legal and none ever
done, and the rulebook's remedy for an entry that will not move was written
for Deferred and Blocked alone. The entry offered a cap on Next or an
extension of the remedy, and preferred the second. Investigation confirmed
the gap in record 0018 and the audits, and found a second beside it: the
remedy the entry cites had never reached the host style's rulebook.

## Decision

Kept, adapted. The remedy extends to Next and becomes decidable: an entry in
Next, Deferred, or Blocked whose text has stood unchanged for two horizons is
promoted, written as a record and removed, or dropped, and every audit reads
the entry's age from history and fails it past two horizons, binding from its
own scope sentence as the family's rule for history-reading checks requires.
The cap was refused for the reason 0018 gave about Now. Each style carries
its own record, and the host's rulebook gains the sentence it lacked.

## Consequences

The reporting project finds its entry adopted at its next re-alignment and
deletes it; its oldest queued items get two horizons from that alignment
before the audit asks what they are for.
