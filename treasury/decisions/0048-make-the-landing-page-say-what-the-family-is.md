# 0048. Make the landing page say what the family is

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

The family's landing page was a catalogue, four rows of topics and
dates and a paragraph on the treasury, under a pitch that still called
the repository one person's custom templates. It said nothing of what
makes the four folders one thing, the law as bytes an audit checks,
the records never edited, the selftests, the upstream road or the
treasury's method, and a visitor who starred the repository did so on
the folders alone. The repository's description and topics said the
same. The owner raised it on 2026-10-10, the project having grown past
the reference it began as.

## Decision

The landing page opens with what the family is and is not, states its
ideas in one line each, lists the styles with what each is and what it
is demonstrated on, says how a style is taken and re-aligned, how the
family keeps itself honest, how feedback travels, what the treasury
holds, and why the family exists, in the owner's voice. It says the
family grows one style at a time, designed from the treasury. The
family audit holds that the page links every seat folder, one carrying
an agent guide at the root, and the treasury, with a plant, so a style
cannot arrive unannounced. The repository's description and topics say
what the page says.

## Consequences

A stranger learns the system before the folders, and a fifth style
arrives on the page in the same change that adds its folder.
