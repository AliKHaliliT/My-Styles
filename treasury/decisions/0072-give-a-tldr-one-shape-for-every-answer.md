# 0072. Give a TLDR one shape for every answer

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-24

## Context

The style's owner asks for a TLDR often and got a different shape each
time, and asked for one shape for any kind of answer, as family law.

## Decision

- A TLDR is three lines of one sentence each, Bottom line, Why and Your
  move, each defined by its job so that it holds for kinds of answer no
  list foresaw, with a task's state line carried word for word. The
  package seat's 0138, the server seat's 0146, the client seat's 0138
  and the host seat's 0133.

## Options considered

- A shape for each kind of answer. Refused, because no list covers every
  answer.

## Consequences

Every worker in the family summarizes the same way, held in review, and
the rules of conduct stay within their budget, since the rules of
landing moved out first.
