# 0060. Scope the every-seat rule to the law every seat mirrors

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-06

## Context

The skills study folded its candidates under one criterion of its
method, that an adoption binds every seat or does not enter. Two rulings
since have refused on that ground by name.
[Treasury 0030, Take eight rules from the skills study and refuse the rest](0030-take-eight-rules-from-the-skills-study-and-refuse-the-rest.md)
refused the rule that a review finding is verified against the current
file because only the host has a review step, and
[treasury 0045, Dispose of five entries from a host-style project at d4b405a](0045-dispose-of-five-entries-from-a-host-style-project-at-d4b405a.md)
declined a ShellCheck step for the server seat's backup script. In
discussion the same ground passed over a lint for the client seat and,
on 2026-11-06, a design linter for it. Yet the seats carry craft of
their own that never met the test and never could. The client seat has
its sliced layers and guarded wire boundary, the server seat its
migration template and its token library, landed this month as the
server seat's 0129 alone, and the host its rules for review records,
screening logs and the judge of a stage, the host seat's 0119 alone.
Each is recorded in its own seat and mirrored nowhere, because the other
seats have nothing to carry. The criterion was written for what every
seat mirrors and was being read over what one seat is.

Measured on 2026-11-06 for this ruling, the server seat is the only
seat that tracks shell, two scripts of ninety-one lines, and ShellCheck
at its default and at its style severity reports nothing on either.

## Decision

The every-seat rule binds shared law. A rule, check or configuration
that lives in the text every seat mirrors, the guides, the rulebooks,
the audits, the gate's protocol, the records law and the prose law,
enters only where every seat can carry it, and lands with a record in
every seat whose bytes it changes. It does not bind seat craft, which is
what a seat carries because of what it is, from its stack and tools to
its architecture and the rules that have a meaning on that seat alone.
Seat craft is judged by the seat's own fold tests, that it adds
decidability or completeness, asks for no taste, lands on its own, and
can be carried by a project built from the seat, and it is recorded in
that seat alone. The test between the two is what the other seats would
do with it. A change they would mirror as text is law, and one that
leaves them nothing to carry is craft.

Under that scope, the two refusals keep their outcome on new ground.

- The review-finding rule of treasury 0030 is refused because no seat
  has a review step that reads a diff. The host's passes read sources
  and each stage names what its decisions were checked against, and the
  family's disposals test every entry against the tree at a commit the
  ruling names. It reopens if a seat gains a review step whose findings
  a diff could mislead.
- The ShellCheck step of treasury 0045 is the server seat's craft and
  passes the seat's tests, and it is refused on the measurement above,
  as treasury 0030 refused the demonstrative rule on one. It reopens
  when the seat's shell grows past its two scripts or a landing fixes a
  defect of the kind the tool names.

## What is refused, and what would reopen it

- Reading the rule as written, over every addition. Refused, because
  the reading was refusing the seats what they already carry, and would
  have refused the server seat's token library had anyone asked. It
  reopens if a piece of seat craft is found mirrored as text in another
  seat, which would make it law.
- Writing the scope into the seats' guides. Refused, because the rule
  governs the family's folding, which a project built from a seat never
  does, and the treasury's index and this ruling hold it. It reopens if
  a seat's guide comes to name the fold tests.
- Deciding the client seat's design linter inside this ruling. Refused,
  because it is that seat's craft and lands, if it lands, as that
  seat's own change on its own measurement, reported beside this ruling
  and not in it. It reopens as a client-seat landing when the
  measurement says so.

## Consequences

A proposal is first sorted, law or craft, and only law meets the
every-seat rule. Two refusals stand on grounds that name what would
reopen them, and the ground that misread two cases is retired. The
server seat keeps its shell unlinted until the measurement changes, and
the client seat's design linter is the first question judged under the
scope.
