# 0075. Dispose of the entry of 2026-11-25 from a client-style project

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-26

## Context

One entry reached the family as the repository's issue 32, dated
2026-11-25, pinned at the family's 48c2f30, which the family's history
holds, and filed as an improvement to every style. It said that the
rules of conduct link the prose law under the name of the file the law
left when the rulebook was split. Tested at the pin and at the family's
2ddae38, the entry was real in every seat and in the arrow's carried
copy, and a scan of the family's Markdown found one more link of the
kind, in the host's guide, and no other.

## Decision

Kept, and widened by the one link the entry did not name. The link's
text names the prose law in the rules of conduct of every seat and in
the host's guide. The package seat's 0143, mirrored into the arrow's
inherited folder, the server seat's 0153, the client seat's 0146 and the
host seat's 0138.

## Options considered

- A check in every docs audit that refuses a link whose text names a
  file other than its target. Refused, because the six links are one
  slip at one split, copied, and each still opened the right page. A
  second slip of the kind reopens it.

## Consequences

The reporting project deletes the entry at its next re-alignment and
takes the rules of conduct as the template carries them.
