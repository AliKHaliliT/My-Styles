# 0049. Take a lab's review technique into the host

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-16

## Context

The owner keeps three modules outside the family, written for a lab's
students and their assistants, one for a literature review, one for
writing a paper and one for reviewing and proofreading it, each a file
of instructions an assistant reads before the work and a legend of every
option and term. The owner asked whether the family could take anything
from them. The reading found one protocol worn three times, a capability
report with fallbacks, a configuration, a legend quoted verbatim, phases
with checkpoints that cannot be batched, a state block for resumption, a
needs block, flags instead of guesses, logs that become a first-person
procedure record, and a pitfalls table, over the standard methodology
literature of systematic reviews. Most of that spine the host already
held, in a stronger form. The evidence levels were its depth words with
an advisory, the preprint rule and the grey tiers its source standing
with a floor, every number a pin and a figure line, the criteria frozen
in an immutable record, the declared shortcuts its collapsed stages, and
the needs block its Left out and Blocked queue. What the host lacked was
the technique inside its six stages, because naming a method was free
and mandating one was priced, so a pass was honest and not good, and the
owner asked that a child be able to run a review of the modules' grade
in any field.

## Options considered

- Keeping the modules outside and handing one to the agent when
  wanted. Refused, because the law makes the demo and the guide what
  an agent cuts from, and what is absent from them is absent from a
  child's work when nobody says otherwise.
- Taking the modules whole, their recipes, their databases and their
  thresholds. Refused, because the recipes are one lab's and the
  host must hold in medicine and law as in engineering; the skeleton
  became law, the technique the house profile a pass follows and
  departs from in writing, and the field-bound tables marked
  paragraphs a project rewrites.
- Taking the modules' provenance log, who wrote each sentence and
  which model did what. Refused, because the family audits the
  discipline and not the author, writes owner or agent as roles and
  never names, and keeps tools out of commit history.
- Taking the student-shaped interaction rules, the fixed turn
  skeleton, the cap of three questions, the severity grades, the
  per-edit meaning flags. Refused, because the report rule says its
  order is not a schema, numbered options already keep a question
  answerable, the family grades nothing, and the commit message
  carries the why of an edit.

## Decision

The host took the technique, each step its own landing with its record,
records 0080 to 0089 of the host. They are the seeds a pass fixes before
its strings and how many they found, the overlap of an extending pass
with the one it extends, a retracted standing with the Checks line
answering for it, a search handed to a person written out whole, a thin
literature as a finding, the nearest review named with what the pass
adds, a screening log per reader beside the record with the raw exports
kept as generated, the method file itself with the demo's field bindings
marked and the sentence that mandated no method replaced, a document
composed from the ledger citing by number and key, and an adopted
inquiry's drafts brought under the ledger, with an exemplar pass owed to
the demo. Every seat took what reached beyond the host, each rule
recorded in the four styles and carried into the arrow. They are a
comment that defers work refused by the linter with a model's leftovers
named by the vocabulary advisory, an invisible character refused in any
tracked text file, a disputed finding re-judged away from the dispute, a
rule quoted before it is explained, an upstream entry that attaches
nothing, a read a tool cut short re-read or named, one spelling variety
across a tree, one term per concept, a verb over the noun made from it,
and an abbreviation expanded at its first use. The modules stay the
owner's, outside the family, where the writing of a paper and its review
belong, beyond the host's institution boundary.

## Consequences

An agent in a child asked for a literature review runs the method and
writes what it skipped, in any field, and the family's own prose and
code gained ten rules a lab had learned the hard way. The owner's
modules and the host now agree on what a pass owes, and an entry
between them travels the upstream road like any other.
