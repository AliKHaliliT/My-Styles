Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-08

## Context

A project built from Keel alone, aligned at `ba37107a9b37`, sent three
entries after moving its compute onto an optional accelerator: a testing rule
for optional backends with fallbacks, a workaround it could not classify in
the tool configuration, and an ambiguity in the rulebook's wording on short
docstrings. Each was tested against the family's own trees.

## Decision

- Kept: both paths of an optional backend are executed and held to a
  recorded tolerance over a fixture that can make them disagree, in the
  test-honesty gate item and the maps of the three artifact styles.
- Kept, and the workaround was the instruction's fault: the adoption section
  now distinguishes a rule selection, which is law, from a binding to the
  demo's stack, which is marked in the style's configuration and freely
  re-adapted or removed. Keel marks its three bindings.
- Kept: the rulebook says a summary of one sentence in the house rhythm,
  where it said one line.

The report arrived nameless, pinned, in the letter shape the family had
asked for on the day before, and it closes with what the project holds
locally; under ruling 0020 that list now lives in the project's own upstream
file rather than in a letter.

## Consequences

The project reverts nothing, keeps its both-paths suite and its tolerance
record as they are, and finds all three entries adopted at its next
re-alignment.
