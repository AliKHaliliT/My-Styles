Status: Accepted

## Context

One project sent the same defect entry from three of its seats, each aligned
at `6dab73e5a818`, under the styles for a package, a server, and a client.
The em dash count had moved into the docs audit the day before with no scope
in history, and its first run outside a workflow reported fourteen records
over the budget across the project's four seats, every one written before
the rule arrived there. A record is immutable beyond its Status line and a
link target, superseding a correct decision over punctuation would falsify
the ledger, and the selftest refused to run while the tree was red, so one
rule the seats could not satisfy took every other rule's proof down with it.

observed that nothing in the records forbade one.

## Decision

Kept. The count over records binds from the arrival of its own scope
sentence, so a record born before the rule reached a tree is never judged
and every editable file is held whatever its age. The selftest proves that a
record over the budget born in the working tree is reported, and the
rehearsal commits one before the audit arrives and requires the child's
audit to pass over it, which is the project's case built and proven. The
records are Keel 0074, ArchetypeCore 0071, Helm 0075, and Quiver 0050, the
arrow inheriting Keel's.

The entry's diagnosis was tested before it was kept. The record that moved
the count names no scope, the family's own trees carry no record over the
budget, and the check had never fired on a record here, which is how the gap
survived its own landing, a rule proven only where its subject was absent.

## Consequences

The project's fourteen records stand as written and its selftest runs again
at its next re-alignment, where it deletes the entry from all three upstream
files. The rehearsal now carries a record over the budget from before the
audit, so the scope is proven where a project would meet it.
