# 0068. Dispose of every record a re-alignment gains

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-17

## Context

Re-alignment told a child to read the records its template gained, recopy
the carried files, sweep what a retired rule required, resolve its upstream
entries, run the gate, and move the pin. A rule the template dropped had a
step that walked the tree and a gate item, Leftovers swept. A rule the
template gained had the verb read, and then whatever the gate happened to
check. So a gained rule with a check went red and was fixed, and a gained
rule held in review bound nothing in the procedure, because reading a record
is not applying it. The delivery gate's hard-rules item re-reads the change
against the review-held clauses, and in a re-alignment the change is the
recopied files, so the child's existing tests, prose, and code were never
the thing re-read.

## Evidence

A project built from the host style, with three arrows under three of the
family's styles, was read by its history on 2026-09-17. Its four
re-alignments between 2026-09-07 and 2026-09-10 changed the carried files,
the upstream and state files, and whatever a check or the project's own
upstream entries demanded. A rule gained on 2026-09-08, that the project's
own decisions folder holds its own decisions and nothing else, was broken in
the commit that brought it, twenty-two of the template's records standing
under the project's numbers in four seats, and the commit body stated the
rule as satisfied. The family's own diagnosis of checks, that the half no
tool reaches rots faster behind a passing signal, described its own
re-alignment procedure, which is a passing signal.

## Options considered

- Leaving the disposition in the commit body. Refused, because commit
  messages are held in review alone and no audit reads one, and a
  disposition written where no check can count it is the sentence this
  record replaces.
- An entry in the upstream file. Refused, because that file holds what the
  child has for its style, is deletion-resolved, and expires; a disposition
  is the child's own decision about a rule and does not expire.
- A check that every gained record changed something in the tree. Refused,
  because a rule that binds nothing in a given tree is a legal outcome, and
  the check would force a change to earn a clean run.
- Exempting no one. Refused for the arrow carried inside its style's
  repository, whose inherited folder moves with every landing under the
  family audit and is aligned at the host's own commit, so nothing there is
  gained by a re-alignment and a disposition would be the maintainer
  disposing of the maintainer's own rule.

## Decision

A re-alignment writes one decision record of the child's own, which links
every record the inherited folder gained, each with its title, and says for
each what in the tree it bound and what changed, or that it bound nothing
and why. The adoption section of every guide carries the step and the gate
gains an item, Records disposed; the rulebook's paragraph on records names
the re-alignment as one of the project's decisions. The docs audit of every
seat holds the enumerable half, that every record the folder gained after
this rule arrived is cited by a record of the project's own, binding from
its own scope sentence like every history-reading check and exempting the
folder's first arrival, which the adoption record stands for whole; whether
a citation is an honest disposition is review's. The check names itself as
not run where the tree has no inherited folder and where the upstream file
aligns an arrow at the host's own commit. Under the ladder the rule takes
the check rung for the citation and the review rung for the disposition,
because no tool reads what a rule did to a tree, and the proof of the check
runs where the folder stood in history, the rehearsal's child, and is named
as skipped in the template's own tree.

## Consequences

A re-alignment costs one record per seat, as long as the list of records
gained, and a reader of a child can see for every rule of the template what
the child did with it and when. A child that recopies the law and runs the
gate without the record goes red on the first gained record. The rules
gained by the re-alignments before this one are not judged, since the check
binds forward, and a child that wants them disposed writes the record for
them by choice.
