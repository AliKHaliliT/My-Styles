# 0058. Choose the form by the shape of the information

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-16

## Context

The owner observed that documents written under the family, above all the
organic documents a project adds for itself, state their information in a
form that is hard to read, continuous prose where a table or a list would
show the same facts, and that the habit recurs however the closing reports
improve. The prose section of the rulebook is the strongest part of the
family, and it is entirely about sentences. Nothing said how to choose
between a paragraph, a list, and a table, and the gate's cognitive-load item
is a judgment item, which binds only a reader who already agrees with it. A
numeric cap on prose was considered and refused before it was measured,
because the pause rule already says a length rule cuts filler and never
information, and a writer satisfies a word cap by dropping the clause that
carried the fact.

## Evidence

Reference density, the count of backticked tokens and links in a prose
paragraph, measured on 2026-09-16 over the family's living documents and over
a project built from the family with forty living documents across a host
and three arrows:

| Measure | The family | The project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prose paragraphs | 447 | 540 |
| Naming at most two references | 80% | 81% |
| Naming at most five | 91% | 93% |
| Naming eight or more | 8 | 16 |
| Naming six or seven | 21 | 22 |
| Mean words per sentence | 24 | 25 |

Every paragraph at eight or more, in both trees, was a schema stated as prose
or a set of bindings: the STATE schema in the rulebook at fifteen, the
docstring convention at twenty, the upstream schema in every guide at eleven,
and in the project a map paragraph describing six pipeline stages and citing
eight decisions in one sentence, thirty-five references, beside a runbook
listing every API key as prose. Eleven of the project's sixteen were the
carried law. The six-and-seven band was mixed in both trees, argued prose
with references in passing beside enumerations of environment variables and
routing tiers. Long sentences clustered in the same paragraphs as the
references.

## Options considered

- A cap on words per sentence or per paragraph. Refused. A writer satisfies
  it by saying less, and the family's own law forbids a length rule that
  cuts information.
- The rule held by review alone, as the cognitive-load item already is.
  Refused as the whole answer. It has not bound so far, and the measurement
  shows the model copies the walls of prose it reads in the law itself.
- A threshold of six references. Refused for now. It fires on argued prose
  in both trees, and an advisory that fires on good prose every run trains
  dismissal.
- A prescribed form per document type. Refused. The organic zone exists so
  a project can write the document it needs, and a schema for a document
  nobody has foreseen is wrong more often than right.

## Decision

The form follows the shape of the information, and the rulebook's prose
section says so: like items that share attributes go in a table, ordered
steps in a numbered list, independent items in bullets, and an argument in a
paragraph, which is for arguing. The docs audits and the host audit advise
when a prose paragraph in a living document names eight or more references,
naming the count and the reform, a list or a table, never a cut, and gate
nothing, because whether a given paragraph enumerates or argues stays with
review. Every selftest plants the case both ways. The family's own dense
paragraphs are reshaped in the same change, the STATE schema and the
docstring sections into tables with rules beneath, the upstream schema and
the host's organic zone into lists, and the four dense paragraphs of the
Helm and Keel maps and READMEs into lists and tables, so the exemplars the
model copies show the form. The docstring convention's rules and every
docstring in every tree are unchanged. Stating the schemas as tables costs
lines a paragraph did not, and the rulebook, at 134 lines before this change,
crossed its budget; it now grows with the law it states, alongside the guide
and the map, because a rulebook must cover whatever rules exist and is
owner-only in every tree.

## Consequences

The advisory fires on nothing in the family and would have named the
project's thirty-five-reference paragraph on the day it was written. A
project re-aligning to the family receives the reshaped law and the
procedure in the same alignment. The threshold is one constant in each
audit, read off two trees and revisable by a ruling when a third disagrees.
The six-and-seven band is left to the procedure, which the writer reads
before writing, and to the weak-model read-back the owner already uses on
rules, which is the test of understandable a check cannot run.
