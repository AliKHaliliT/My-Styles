# 0096. Keep an entry's anonymity where a tool can decide it

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-09

## Context

An upstream entry is written to leave the repository, and the guide
already says it names nothing that identifies the project, no name, no
person, no host, no path or address, and no fact about its domain
beyond what the entry needs, whatever the project's visibility. The
rule bound by review alone, and it left two things unsaid. The part
describing how the work surfaced the entry is where a project's
subject creeps in, and an entry quotes the template's bytes and never
the project's own. With entries now travelling as issues on a
public repository, and an agent able to write one, the owner's reading
before it leaves and a tool's refusal of the commonest leaks were the
two forms still missing.

## Evidence

Measured on 2026-10-09. Every seat's audit held the entry's shape, its
kind, its pin, its four parts and its horizon, and nothing in it read
the entry's words. An address, a URL, an email and an absolute path
are patterns a tool decides without reading for meaning; a project's
name or a person's is not, and stays with review.

## Options considered

- A tool that reads an entry for names. Refused, because no pattern
  tells a project's name from a word, and a check that guesses would
  advise on every entry or on none.
- Keeping the rule as a sentence alone. Refused, because the sentence
  bound an agent only as far as it read, and a URL pasted from a
  terminal is the leak that needs no intent.

## Decision

The upstream block says that the part describing how the work
surfaced the entry names the kind of work and never its subject, that
an entry quotes the template's bytes and never the project's own code,
data or prose, that whatever cannot be said in public stays in the
project's private ledger, and that the owner reads an entry as a
stranger would before it leaves the repository. Every seat's audit
refuses an entry carrying an address, a URL, an email or an absolute
path, with a plant for each, and the ledger carries the row. A name
stays review's, and the guide says so.

## Consequences

The leaks a tool can see are refused before an entry is written into
the file, and the ones it cannot see have an owner's eyes on them at
the one moment that matters, the moment the entry leaves.
