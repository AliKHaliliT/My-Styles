# 0106. Re-judge a disputed finding away from the dispute

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

An agent that reports a finding and hears the owner object re-judges
it in the context that heard the objection, where the objection is the
newest thing said and the finding is the oldest, and what comes back
is a second judgment of the argument rather than of the evidence. The
guide said how a question is posed and how an off-option reply is
restated, and nothing about what happens when the owner pushes back
on a finding. A review method the owner keeps outside the family
reassesses a disputed issue only in a fresh session that is given the
issue and the evidence and never the rebuttal, and says why, because a
reviewer that changes its mind under pressure is measuring the
pressure.

## Options considered

- A fresh session for every dispute. Refused, because most disputes
  end with the owner deciding, which is the owner's right, and a
  session spun up to confirm that is a cost without a reader.
- Leaving it as a practice. Refused, because a practice lives in one
  person's memory and the next agent in the tree never heard of it.

## Decision

A finding the owner disputes is not re-judged in the context that
heard the dispute. It goes to a reader who sees the finding and its
evidence and not the exchange, another session or the owner, and the
owner's decision ends it either way. The sentence stands in every
guide's pause bullet, held in review, because no check can see a
judgment move.

## Consequences

A reversal under argument is visible as what it is, and a finding
that survives a fresh reader survived something.
