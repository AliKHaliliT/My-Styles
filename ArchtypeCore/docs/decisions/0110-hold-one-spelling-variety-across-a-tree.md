# 0110. Hold one spelling variety across a tree

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

The spelling advisory read misspellings and nothing else, so a tree
carried both spellings of one word with no check to notice. Measured
with the variant dictionary the rule installs, over every tracked file
outside the records, the studies and the audits, the other variety stood
at nineteen living sites in fifteen files. They were one rulebook
sentence repeated across the five seats, one guide bullet repeated
across four guides, one comment repeated across three lint
configurations and a test configuration, two docstrings, a word the
host's rulebook spelled both ways, three times the other, and a
possessive the dictionary misses in the same rulebook. The records that
carry the other variety stand as written, since a record admits no edit.
A review method the owner keeps outside the family asks for one spelling
variety per document at setup and holds the manuscript to it.

## Options considered

- Leaving the variety to each writer. Refused, because the two
  spellings of one word in one tree are the kind of drift a reader
  reads as carelessness, and nothing decides between them without a
  rule.
- British English. Refused, because the tree is American by a wide
  count and the demos' stacks spell their own identifiers that way.
- A gate rather than advice. Refused, because a quoted source keeps
  its own spelling and the tool cannot tell a quotation from prose.

## Decision

One spelling variety holds across a tree, American English in this
family, so a document never carries both spellings of one word, and a
quoted source keeps its own spelling inside its quotation marks. Every
spelling advisory reads codespell's variant dictionary beside its
defaults, so a word in the other variety is named with the misspellings,
where the tool is installed, and a real term of the domain or a quoted
spelling is named in `.codespellignore` like any other; the host names
grey, the field's own spelling for literature outside the reviewed
channels and the word its standing law uses. The nineteen living sites
were corrected, the arrow's two among them with its claims re-verified,
since its configuration and its source are evidence paths, and the host
audit's one message that spelled a word the other way went with them.
The rule took the form of an advisory, because the tool decides the word
and not the quotation.

## Consequences

A word has one spelling in living prose, and a British form that
arrives is named on the next run rather than copied by the next
writer.
